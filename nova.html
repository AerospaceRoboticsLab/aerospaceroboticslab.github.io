Open-source hardware

NOVA
an open planetary rover

NOVA is a four-wheel, independently steered research rover designed and built in the AstroX Lab for testing planetary autonomy on our lunar-analog testbed. Everything needed to build one — CAD, bill of materials, and build notes — is released here so other labs and student teams can rebuild, modify, and extend it.

Build guide

Rebuild it in four steps

  1. Order the parts. The bill of materials below lists every purchased component with a supplier link. Most items ship from Amazon, DigiKey, Robotis, and Stereolabs.
  2. Print and machine. Chassis tubs, lids, battery case, Jetson housing, ZED mounts, and swerve-module parts are 3D-printed in PLA; the screw-plate templates and bracket are cut from 6061 aluminum plate. Install the M2.5–M5 heat-set inserts before assembly.
  3. Assemble. Open NOVA_V2_Assembly.SLDASM (from the GitHub repo ↗) in SolidWorks for the full exploded structure: four swerve modules, the differential bar, and the chassis sub-assemblies.
  4. Wire and flash. The Pico 2W drives the Cytron motor drivers and reads the IMU; the Jetson Orin Nano runs perception on the ZED 2i and talks to the Dynamixels through the U2D2 hub. Software release coming soon.
